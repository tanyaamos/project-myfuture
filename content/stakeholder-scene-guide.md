# Project MyFuture — Scene Flow Guide

**For stakeholders · Arizona State University admissions experience**

---

## The big idea

This is a scroll-driven admissions story, not a traditional website. A student moves through cinematic chapters. Along the way they make **three choices** that personalize what they see:

1. **Interest** — what pulls them in  
2. **Learning style** — how they learn best  
3. **Campus** — where they see themselves  

Those choices shape headlines, stats, programs, imagery, and links for the rest of the journey.

---

## One-line flow

**Inspire → choose your path → show your future → pick how & where you learn → feel the place → prove it with real students & programs → affirm belonging → recap your story → invite a visit → close with pride.**

---

## Act 1 — Hook & invitation

### Scene 1 — Opening *(includes former Scene 2 copy)*

**Purpose:** Set the emotional tone and lower the pressure.

- Opens on a cinematic hero image
- First message: *"Your future doesn't start with choosing a university. It starts with imagining what's possible."*
- As they scroll, that text fades and the next lines appear in the same spot: *"Some people arrive knowing exactly where they're going. Most don't. Either way… you're in the right place."*

**Stakeholder framing:** We lead with possibility and reassurance, not rankings or application pressure.

### Scene 3 — What pulls you in?

**Purpose:** First interactive moment — start personalization.

- Bright section with floating cards: Build things, Help people, Create, Change the world, and more
- Student must pick one to continue (or browse degrees and come back)

**Stakeholder framing:** This is the fork in the road. Everything after can reflect their curiosity.

---

## Act 2 — Your path takes shape

### Scene 4 — Personalized mirror

**Purpose:** Confirm their choice and make the story feel "about them."

- Full-screen transition: headline, color, and imagery shift based on interest
- Example feel: *"You're drawn to building things — here's what that looks like at ASU."*

### Scene 5 — Path reveal

**Purpose:** Show 3 cinematic "moments" on their path.

- Horizontal scroll through tailored stories — labs, studios, ventures, etc.

**Stakeholder framing:** Proof of life, not a degree list.

### Scene 6 — Momentum

**Purpose:** One big emotional stat + closing beat for Act 2.

- A single powerful number and line tied to their interest

**Stakeholder framing:** Leave them feeling "this could be real for me."

---

## Act 3 — Deeper personalization

### Scene 11 — How do you learn best?

**Purpose:** Second choice — refine the story.

- Options: Hands-on, With people, On my own, Still exploring

**Stakeholder framing:** Moves from *what* they care about to *how* they thrive.

### Scene 12 — Where do you see yourself?

**Purpose:** Third choice — campus fit.

- Tempe, Downtown Phoenix, Polytechnic, West Valley, ASU Online, or Not sure yet
- Cards link out to real campus and visit info

**Stakeholder framing:** Place becomes part of the narrative, not a dropdown menu.

---

## Act 4 — Place as character

### Scene 13 — Campus immersion

**Purpose:** Cinematic hero moment for their chosen campus.

- Full-screen pinned scene with vibe word + headline (e.g. Tempe: **Energy — "The heartbeat of ASU."**)

**Stakeholder framing:** Viewbook-style campus storytelling.

### Scene 14 — Arizona

**Purpose:** Expand the setting — Arizona as part of the experience.

- Scroll through landscape stats: sunny days, Sedona, Grand Canyon, national parks

**Stakeholder framing:** Lifestyle and place, not just buildings.

### Scene 15 — Unexpected places

**Purpose:** Show ASU's reach beyond Arizona.

- Horizontal scroll: Antarctica, LA, London, Bermuda, D.C., and more

**Stakeholder framing:** Global opportunities — study abroad, centers, real programs.

---

## Act 5 — Proof & belonging

*These run after place/immersion so the story builds: who you are → where you are → why ASU is real.*

### Scene 7 — Student voice

**Purpose:** Human proof — a real student quote and photo.

- Golden Conversations / viewbook feel

### Scene 8 — Stat beats

**Purpose:** Credibility in cinematic form.

- Three full-screen proof points, one number at a time
- Content adapts to interest + learning style + campus

### Scene 9 — Real experiences

**Purpose:** Named programs and opportunities.

- Dreamscape Learn, Venture Devils, MIX Center, etc. — tailored to their path

### Scene 10 — You belong

**Purpose:** Emotional close to the "proof" chapter.

- Personalized *"you belong here"* moment before the recap

---

## Act 6 — Recap & soft conversion

### Scene 16 — Future snapshot

**Purpose:** Shareable recap — "Spotify Wrapped" for their ASU path.

- Builds a personal card: interest, learning style, campus, programs, hero stat, quote
- Share button for clipboard or native share on mobile

### Scene 17 — Picture yourself

**Purpose:** Soft visit CTA.

- *"If you're starting to picture yourself here — See Tempe for yourself."* (campus-aware)
- Links to real Experience ASU tour pages

### Scene 18 — Next step

**Purpose:** Four clear doors, not one hard sell.

- Visit campus, explore campus online, browse degrees, connect with admission / @FutureSunDevils

### Scene 19 — Sendoff

**Purpose:** Warm close.

- *"Thank you, Sun Devils."* + #FutureSunDevil

**Stakeholder framing:** Brand warmth, not a form submission wall.

Scene 2 (campus life) is no longer a separate section. Its copy now lives inside Scene 1 as the second text beat on the same hero image.

## Demo tips

- **Scene 3** — first interaction; everything after reacts to this  
- **Scenes 11 & 12** — second and third layers of personalization  
- **Scene 16** — strong "wow" moment (shareable recap)  
- **Scenes 17–18** — soft conversion with real ASU links, not a form wall
