# Real experiences

Copy for Scene 9. Three programs stack on the page. The set depends on the interest chosen in “What pulls you in?”

**Kicker:** Real experiences

**Headline (before all three choices):** Not brochures. Actual programs. Actual degrees.  
**Headline (interest, learning style, and campus all selected):** Degrees for your path. Specific majors matched to you.

Each panel shows the program name, a headline, a description, a degree name, and the link **Explore this degree on degrees.asu.edu**.

A campus choice replaces one of the three panels. Those replacements are at the bottom.
